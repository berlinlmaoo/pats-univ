## Result

All accounts use **Home · Events · Card · Community · Account** / **Beranda · Event · Kartu · Komunitas · Akun**. Account exposes Admin mode only for authorized staff. The switch follows server `user.adminScope`, remembers the same signed-in account/device, resets on sign-out/account change, and does not grant permissions or membership. Personal participation follows actual membership, including staff with their own member profile; staff-only accounts get an honest Card empty state. Active staff scan/card tasks guard mode switching until finished or cancelled. Mode-on staff work is grouped around an event workspace, with event/city context pinned outside the scrolling content. Normal Card always means personal identity.

Home and event detail share the deliberately tapped **next event → poster → reservation → check-in → completed** journey. The first three steps use explicit taps and the idempotent `/api/me/journey` API; Home consumes `/api/me/home`. Pending reservations remain pending. Check-in is server-confirmed, and completed requires a recorded check-in plus an ended event. The isolated Share poster placeholder records its approved tap without publishing, tracking shares or awarding XP. Authentication remains email OTP. FDC remains an explicit dash pending its definition.

The new supplied KTM front/back replace the old artwork. NAME, FAVORITE DRINK and QOTD (`face.quote`, with profile `quotes` fallback) use uppercase licensed Avenir Black values, with white QR modules within the maroon box. Rendering is isolated in `KtmArtwork.kt`; geometry is replaceable in `assets/ktm-layout.json`; artwork is in `res/drawable-nodpi/ktm_{front,back}_current.png`. No artwork was redrawn. Card consumes separate `/api/me/card` digital readiness and physical preparation fields and does not invent a staff NIM or XP balance.
